    How to Play

    A daily, competitive, one-thumb parcel-delivery game.

    The goal: Pick up cargo from every W warehouse, then deliver it all to the D drop-off as fast as you can.

    Controls: Hold to steer right, release to steer left.
    Tap and hold anywhere on screen (mobile), or hold spacebar or the mouse button (desktop).

    The job. You're a truck driver on the clock: your boss wants every parcel delivered, and fast. Start at the base, pick up cargo from each W warehouse, then bring it all to the D drop-off.

    How you win. Two things decide your time: finding the most efficient route that visits every warehouse before the drop-off, and driving that route cleanly.

    Steering, one input. Your truck always curves left on its own. Hold to steer right; release to drift back left. That's the whole scheme: tap and hold anywhere on screen (mobile), or use spacebar or the mouse button (desktop).

    A fresh track every day. Each day brings a brand-new road network, warehouses in new spots, and a new color scheme, and everyone races the same daily track.

    Compete. Every day has its own global leaderboard. Tap any of the top 10 to race against a "ghost" replay of their run.

    Read the terrain.

    • ROAD is fastest: stay on it when you can.
    • GRASS is slower, but cutting a corner across it can still save time.
    • MUD slows you down and makes the truck slide: avoid it.
    • ROCKS are deadly: clipping one will almost certainly throw off your cargo.

    Mind your cargo. Watch its color: when it turns red, it's about to fall off. Ease off and drive smoothly to let it settle and recover.

    Getting around.

    Restart or quit mid-run: the top-left menu on mobile, or Backspace to restart and Escape to quit on desktop.

    Replay earlier days: browse up to 7 days back from the main menu.

    Switch between the daily map and the much larger weekly challenge with the Daily / Weekly toggle at the top. On the weekly map, a small arrow by the truck points you to the next warehouse (green once they're all done, pointing to the drop-off).
